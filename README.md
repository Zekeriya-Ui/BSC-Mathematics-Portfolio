# BSC Mathematics Portfolio

Welcome to my undergraduate mathematics journey! This repository showcases my academic progress, coursework, projects, and mathematical explorations throughout my BSC in Mathematics degree.

## 📚 Overview

This portfolio documents my development as a mathematician, from foundational concepts to advanced theoretical and applied mathematics. It includes:

- **Coursework & Modules** — Solutions and summaries of key courses
- **Projects & Research** — Independent and collaborative mathematical investigations
- **Problem Sets & Solutions** — Organized collections of assignments and solutions
- **Visualizations & Implementations** — Code and graphics illustrating mathematical concepts
- **Reflections & Learning Notes** — Personal insights and academic growth

## 🎯 Academic Goals

- Master core mathematical disciplines (Analysis, Algebra, Geometry)
- Develop problem-solving and proof-writing skills
- Apply mathematics to real-world problems
- Explore specialized areas of interest
- Build a strong foundation for graduate studies
