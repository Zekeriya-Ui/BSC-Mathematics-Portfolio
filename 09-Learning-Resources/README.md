## 📖 Recommended Textbooks

### Foundational
- "Calculus" by James Stewart
- "Linear Algebra Done Right" by Sheldon Axler
- "Discrete Mathematics" by Kenneth Rosen

### Core Mathematics
- "Principles of Mathematical Analysis" by Walter Rudin
- "Abstract Algebra" by Dummit and Foote
- "Introduction to Topology" by Mendelson

### Applied Mathematics
- "Numerical Analysis" by Burden, Faires, Burden
- "Probability and Statistics" by Morris DeGroot
- "Differential Equations" by Boyce and DiPrima

### Advanced Topics
- "Complex Analysis" by Lars Ahlfors
- "Functional Analysis" by Rudin
- "Partial Differential Equations" by Lawrence Evans

## 🎯 Study Tips

- **Active Learning** — Engage with material through problem-solving
- **Concept Mapping** — Link related ideas and theories
- **Multiple Representations** — View concepts from different angles
- **Teaching Others** — Explain concepts to solidify understanding
- **Regular Review** — Spaced repetition for retention
